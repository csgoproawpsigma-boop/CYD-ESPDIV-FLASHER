Chrome / Edge / Opera on desktop. Safari and Firefox won't work. On phones: Android with an OTG cable and Chrome only.

How to flash

  1. Connect the CYD with a data cable.Some USB cables are charge-only. If no port shows up, swap the cable first.
  2. Pick a version and press Flash.The browser lists serial ports. Look for CH340, CP210x or "USB Serial".
  3. Press Install and wait.Usually 30–60 seconds. Don't unplug until it says "Installation complete".
  4. Press RESET on the board.Screen still black? Unplug and replug USB.

Troubleshooting

No port shows up in the list

Nine times out of ten it's a charge-only cable. Otherwise install the CH340 or CP210x driver. On Linux, add yourself to the dialout group.

Stuck on "Connecting…"

Hold the BOOT button on the board, press Flash, and keep holding BOOT until writing starts. Some CYD boards don't enter download mode on their own.

Flashed, but the screen is white or black

Press RESET. If that doesn't help, flash again with "Erase device" on. CYD boards ship with different display drivers (ILI9341 and ST7789), so the firmware has to be built for yours.

Can I flash from my phone?

Only Android, with an OTG cable and Chrome. iPhones don't support Web Serial at all.

Is it safe for the board?

Yes. You're writing to the normal ESP32 flash, same as any other firmware. Worst case, you flash it again.
